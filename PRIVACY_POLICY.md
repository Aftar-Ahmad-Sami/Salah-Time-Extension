# Privacy Policy for Salah Time Extension

## Introduction
The **Salah Time Extension** respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information that you may provide while using the extension. This document outlines what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

## Information We Collect
The Salah Time Extension may collect the following information:
- **Location Data**: The extension uses your device's geolocation to fetch accurate prayer times based on your current location. This data is not stored or shared with any third parties.

## How We Use Your Information
The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:
- To calculate and display accurate prayer times based on your location.
- To display your current location details using the OpenStreetMap Nominatim API.

## Data Sharing
The Salah Time Extension does not share your personal data with any third parties. Location data is sent to the following APIs to fetch prayer times and location details:
- **AlAdhan API**: For fetching Islamic prayer timings.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim API**: For reverse geocoding to retrieve location details.

## Data Storage
The Salah Time Extension saves your settings, your last known location (or the city you entered) and today's prayer times in your browser's local storage on your device, so the popup can open quickly. This data never leaves your device except as described above, and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions
The extension requires the following permissions:
- **Geolocation**: To determine your current location for accurate prayer times.

## Your Rights
As a user, you have the right to:
- Disable location access for the extension through your browser settings.
- Uninstall the extension at any time if you no longer wish to use it.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the repository's documentation.
